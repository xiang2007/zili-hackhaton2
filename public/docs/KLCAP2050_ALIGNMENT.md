# Alignment with Kuala Lumpur climate goals

This crosswalk is a policy handoff, not an assertion that the dashboard alone delivers the policy outcome.

| Policy direction | Dashboard contribution | Suggested measure | Evidence owner | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| KLCAP2050: Cooler City / reduced urban heat-island risk | Baseline and scenario LST views; repeatable thresholds | Number/share of evidence cells and telecom records ≥38 °C; scenario change | GIS/model custodian | Telecom observations are not a citywide population sample |
| KLCAP2050: increased resilience against climate hazards | Scenario stress test and asset-continuity shortlist | High-risk asset count under agreed warming assumptions | Telecom engineering + resilience team | Requires engineering condition, power and backhaul data |
| KLCAP2050: monitoring, evaluation and reporting | Versioned exports, summary JSON, release checksums | Data vintage, model version, dashboard release, decision log completeness | Product owner | No central audit store in this static release |
| PSKL2040 Goal 3: Green, Healthy and Vibrant | Cross-reference heat priority with canopy/green interventions | Priority cells with funded canopy/cooling action | Landscape/environment departments | No canopy/intervention inventory was supplied |
| PSKL2040 Goal 4: Climate Smart and Low Carbon | Shared multi-agency evidence and scenario comparison | Agencies trained; scenarios reviewed; actions closed | Interagency task force | Needs formal governance and recurring review cadence |

## Reporting rhythm

Monthly operational reporting should record data freshness, scenario assumptions, number of candidate screens, unresolved gates, and mitigation actions. Quarterly governance review should approve any threshold, model, data, or scoring change before deployment. Annual policy review should assess whether monitored indicators still match the KLCAP/PSKL implementation framework.
