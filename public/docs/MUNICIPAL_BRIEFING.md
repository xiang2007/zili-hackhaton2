# DBKL · MBSA · MBPJ stakeholder briefing

## Meeting outcome

Agree whether to proceed with a controlled pilot, confirm the decision boundary, assign data/product owners, select validation areas, and approve a 90-day iteration plan.

## 45-minute run-of-show

| Time | Topic | Demonstration / decision |
|---:|---|---|
| 0–5 min | Shared heat problem | Portfolio baseline and UHVI reference |
| 5–12 min | Evidence quality | Source lineage, thresholds, exposure-versus-vulnerability distinction |
| 12–22 min | Scenario lab | +2 °C stress, 1 °C cooling, optional supplied model delta |
| 22–30 min | Approval workflow | Place candidate, adjust weights, export board memo |
| 30–37 min | Public-health/civil-defense use | Cross-reference action matrix; explain prohibited uses |
| 37–42 min | KLCAP/PSKL alignment | Measures, owners, reporting cadence |
| 42–45 min | Decisions | Pilot areas, owners, dates, acceptance criteria |

## Agency-specific discussion

### DBKL

- Validate terminology and measures against KLCAP2050 and PSKL2040 implementation reporting.
- Supply authoritative Kuala Lumpur boundaries, planning zones, land use, canopy/green interventions, facilities, and approved climate layers.
- Nominate product, GIS/data, planning/legal, public-health, and resilience owners.

### MBSA

- Select Shah Alam validation areas representing urban core, industrial, residential, and peri-urban conditions.
- Confirm planning-review gates and local cooling/greening inventories.
- Test cross-boundary continuity and common export fields.

### MBPJ

- Select Petaling Jaya validation areas with dense infrastructure and heat-exposed corridors.
- Test board workflow speed, explainability, accessibility, and the usefulness of proposed-site proximity measures.
- Identify local health/facility and canopy datasets suitable for lawful join.

### Public health / civil defense

- Define actions that can be triggered only after current meteorological warning and population/facility cross-checks.
- Validate language to avoid confusing land-surface temperature with air temperature or clinical risk.
- Confirm escalation roles, alert ownership, and after-action reporting.

## Decisions to record

1. Pilot status and scope.
2. Named accountable product owner and data custodians.
3. Authoritative replacement data for administrative boundaries and UHVI geometry.
4. Scenario assumptions and validation method.
5. User groups and accessibility/security review.
6. Success measures and review date.
