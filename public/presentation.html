Predictive scenario tool

The scoring rule is visible and contestable.

scenario LST = baseline + regional heat − cooling + optional greening delta

composite = thermal resilience × weight + coverage gap × weight

Default: 60% thermal resilience / 40% coverage gap. The nearest 12 supplied telecom records estimate local LST. Reviewers can change weights and the exported memo records the assumptions.
