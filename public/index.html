What this dashboard can—and cannot—decide

It supports heat screening and scenario comparison across the supplied telecom observations. Low, Medium, and High use the source breakpoints <32 °C, 32–<38 °C, and ≥38 °C.

The smooth default surface is generated from v6 area-level mean LST values. The supplied baseline PDF remains a cartographic reference; exact interactive values come from the v6 polygons and derived evidence grid.

The UHVI view combines normalised LST, income vulnerability and older-population share across 56 constituencies. The separate thermal exposure grid remains a telecom-focused heat proxy and should not be confused with UHVI.

Proposed-site scores combine a lower-temperature preference with distance to the nearest supplied telecom record. They are preliminary and must pass the approval gates.
